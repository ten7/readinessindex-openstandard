This is an open standard, not a product. It was created by TEN7 and is published under CC BY-SA 4.0. Anyone may read it, implement it and propose changes, and a score computed from it can be reproduced from what is written here.

TEN7 is the company that created the standard, and readinessindex.io is a product of TEN7: a scanner that implements it. They are linked so you know where the standard came from, not as a recommendation. This site does not depend on either.
