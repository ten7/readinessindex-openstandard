How the points add up, and where they round

Each test scores a band from 0 to 4. What it contributes is its weight times its band, divided by four, so a test worth 2 at band 3 contributes exactly 1.5 points. The score is every contribution added at full precision, divided by the 100 points above, and rounded once at the end.

Every points figure a report shows is a whole number, because a report is meant to be read aloud and nobody argues about three quarters of a point. That means the rows of a report will not add up to the score on it: roughly a third of them round up or down to get there. Both figures are right, and the score is always computed from the exact ones, never from the rounded ones.

A report should let the reader check that. The accounting, with every test, its band, the arithmetic written out and the final division, shows nothing rounded.
