# Code of conduct

This is a place to argue about a standard, and arguments are welcome. People are not the subject.

- **Disagree with the claim, not the person.** "This citation does not say that" is useful. Anything about who somebody is, is not.
- **Bring evidence.** A proposal, an objection and a correction are all stronger with a source.
- **Assume good faith, and ask when unsure.** A short question usually settles what a long reply would not.
- **No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks, and no advertising.** A link to your own product is fine when it answers the question that was asked. A pitch is not.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for the standard. Support for a particular scanner belongs with its vendor.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads and block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or write to the maintainers listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).
